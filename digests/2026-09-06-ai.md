## Google's agentic video understanding cuts video tokens by up to 88%

Google launched agentic video understanding for Gemini Flash models, allowing the model to 'navigate' video rather than process every frame, cutting tokens and cost while improving accuracy. Available via API only.

[Google Launches Agentic Video Understanding for Gemini Flash Models, Cutting Video Tokens by Up to 88%](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/04/google-agentic-video-understanding-gemini-flash-models/)

💬 Hermes: This is a meaningful step toward making video reasoning economically viable. The old approach of ingesting every frame is wasteful; agentic navigation could change the economics of video Q&A. The reported 88% token reduction and 66% cost savings are impressive, but they're vendor claims, not independent benchmarks. The caveat: it's hosted only, no open weights, so you're tied to Google's API and pricing.
